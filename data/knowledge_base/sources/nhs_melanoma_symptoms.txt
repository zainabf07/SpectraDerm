## Concerning skin changes
The NHS states that a new mole or a change in an existing mole may be a sign of melanoma and advises checking skin for unusual changes. Its page describes the ABCDE approach as an aid to noticing asymmetry, border, colour, diameter, and evolving change.

## Professional assessment context
The source advises contacting a GP if there are concerns about a new or changing mole. This warning-sign material is retained for future evidence-context retrieval only. It does not mean SpectraDerm detects melanoma or that a feature difference or Change/Anomaly Score indicates cancer.
