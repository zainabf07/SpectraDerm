## Summary
Pigmentation means coloring. Skin pigmentation disorders affect skin color. Melanin is the pigment that contributes to skin color, and changes affecting melanin production can affect patches of skin or larger areas.

## Pigmentation changes
Too much melanin can make skin darker, while too little can make skin lighter. The MedlinePlus topic page notes that sun exposure, pregnancy, and some conditions can be associated with darker skin, while infections, blisters, and burns can be associated with lighter skin. This source is contextual information about visible pigmentation; it is not a method for identifying a condition from an image or feature value.
