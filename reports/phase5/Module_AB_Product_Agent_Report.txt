SpectraDerm Module AB: Product Agent
=====================================

Objective and narrow scope
--------------------------
AB provides deterministic suggestions of general OTC skincare categories from
supplied model-derived appearance/pattern context. Recommendations are general
OTC skincare categories and are not diagnoses, prescriptions, or
disease-treatment plans. AB does not inspect images, call models, retrieve
evidence, or recalculate monitoring.

Architecture and upstream relationships
----------------------------------------
AB accepts opaque structured outputs from X Vision, Y Monitoring, Z Evidence,
and AA Safety. It uses X-style finding text only to recognize one controlled
appearance category and retains Y monitoring context without reinterpreting
scores. Z is not queried. AA is the hard gate: when its professional assessment
flag is true, AB returns referral_preferred and no recommendations.

Controlled catalog and mappings
-------------------------------
The catalog contains category-only entries for gentle cleansing, moisturizing,
sun protection, pigment-support/corrective context, topical antioxidants,
gentle chemical exfoliation, soothing skincare, and selected OTC acne-like/
texture skincare categories. The only supported appearance categories are
pigmentation, redness_irritation, acne_like_texture, dryness_barrier, and
unknown. Explicit pattern mappings give different lists for each supported
pattern; unknown has no targeted recommendation. Entries contain no brands,
products, prices, URLs, affiliate content, or commercial partners.

Safety, separation, and validation
-----------------------------------
Reasons identify only a model-derived appearance/pattern context; they do not
claim a category cures, treats, or diagnoses a condition. Persistent or
increasing model-derived change alone does not trigger referral. Commercial
products do not influence SpectraDerm's detection, reconstruction, feature
extraction, ML scoring, anomaly analysis, or monitoring. Synthetic tests cover
all mappings, distinct outputs, AA gating, no referral from change alone,
non-fabrication, no brands/prescriptions, preservation, and determinism.

Limitations and future integration
----------------------------------
AB cannot establish suitability for an individual, offer a regimen, or replace
professional care. A future AD orchestrator may consume its structured result;
AB does not implement product shopping, referral, MCP, backend, or frontend.
