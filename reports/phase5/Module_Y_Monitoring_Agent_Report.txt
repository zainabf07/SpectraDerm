SpectraDerm Module Y: Monitoring Agent
=======================================

Objective
---------
Coordinate and interpret existing Module Q, R, and S outputs with supplied
historical records. Module Y does not reproduce any of their numerical
algorithms and does not provide diagnosis, disease probability, or risk.

Relationship to Q/R/S, inputs, and outputs
-------------------------------------------
Module R baseline/reference information, Module S current comparison, and
Module Q change/anomaly output are accepted as already-computed opaque inputs.
HistoricalScanRecord preserves scan ID, timestamp, optional change score,
status, and summary. MonitoringAgentResult keeps raw inputs in evidence_inputs
separate from current status, trend, scores, summary, limitations, and safety
message.

Historical handling and trend logic
-----------------------------------
Records are ordered deterministically by timestamp then scan ID. Only finite
supplied change scores are valid. Fewer than two valid scores produces
insufficient_history. With three or more valid scores, every successive score
must be strictly greater for increasing_change. Otherwise, if maximum minus
minimum is at most 2.0 fixed engineering score points, the trend is stable;
all other cases are changed. Two scores can never produce increasing_change.
The score range is an engineering consistency rule, not a clinical threshold.

Safety boundaries and limitations
---------------------------------
An increase refers only to the supplied model-derived change score and is not
proof of disease progression. Scores are never converted into disease or
cancer probability, medical severity, clinical risk, or diagnosis. Estimated
spectral information is not measured spectroscopy. Missing values remain
missing, and professional assessment is not recommended merely because a score
increases. The agent cannot validate upstream records or establish a trend
from insufficient history.

Deterministic validation and future integration
-----------------------------------------------
Tests use synthetic records for insufficient, stable, changed, increasing, and
non-monotonic histories, timestamp ordering, missing fields, raw Q/S
preservation, determinism, and safety boundaries. Its structured result is
intended for future composition with Vision Agent X, Evidence Agent Z, Safety
Agent AA, and a later Orchestrator AD; none of those future agents is created
here.
