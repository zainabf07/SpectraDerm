Observed Pattern
----------------
The reconstructed spectral curve generally follows the ground-truth
spectral shape. Agreement is stronger in the lower/middle VIS range,
while larger deviations appear toward approximately 610-700 nm.

Limitations
-----------
This is a preliminary prototype experiment using only 10 training
pairs and 3 held-out samples. The results are not a clinical accuracy
claim and should not be interpreted as disease-detection performance.

The reconstructed spectra are AI-estimated spectra, not measured
spectra. Spectral reconstruction error and anomaly scores must not be
interpreted as disease probability.
