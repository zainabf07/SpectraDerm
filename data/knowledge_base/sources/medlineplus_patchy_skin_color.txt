## Considerations
Patchy skin color describes irregular lighter or darker areas. The MedlinePlus Medical Encyclopedia describes mottling as a patchy appearance related to blood vessel changes.

## Context and professional assessment
The source notes that irregular color can be associated with melanin changes, blood vessel changes, or inflammation related to rashes. It advises contacting a health professional for lasting skin color changes without a known cause, a new mole or growth, or an existing growth that changes color, size, or appearance. These are general source-context statements and do not establish what any SpectraDerm feature or score means.
