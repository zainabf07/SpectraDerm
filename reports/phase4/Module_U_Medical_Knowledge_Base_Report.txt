SpectraDerm Module U Step 1: Curated Medical Knowledge Base
=============================================================

Purpose
-------
Module U Step 1 creates a small, local, attribution-preserving corpus for a
future RAG layer. It contains evidence/context only; it is not a diagnostic
system and does not map SpectraDerm features to disease labels.

Sources and scope
-----------------
The corpus contains exactly seven approved official sources: MedlinePlus pages
on skin pigmentation disorders and patchy skin color; American Academy of
Dermatology pages on dark spots, rosacea, atopic dermatitis, and acne; and the
NHS page on melanoma symptoms. Topics are limited to pigmentation, visible
color/redness or vascular appearance, inflammation-related appearance,
surface/texture appearance, limited common-condition context, and warning
signs/professional-assessment context.

Implementation
--------------
Each locally cached cleaned source note has a deterministic source_id and a
manifest entry with title, organization, official URL, topic/category, and
retrieved_at. Chunk IDs are deterministic source_id:chunk-NNNN identifiers.
The chunker uses ``##`` section headings and paragraph boundaries first, with a
500-token target and 75-token overlap when splitting is required. Every chunk
retains its source attribution metadata.

Safety and limitations
----------------------
Source discussion of a condition does not mean SpectraDerm detects that
condition. The corpus supplies retrieval context only; it contains no disease
probabilities, clinical thresholds, or claims that RGB-derived estimated
spectra are measured biomarkers. It does not add claims about collagen,
water/NIR, or unsupported characteristics. Embeddings, vector search, RAG
generation, agents, MCP, and user-facing systems are outside this step.

Validation
----------
Tests check source registration, required metadata, deterministic IDs,
non-empty cleaned text/chunks, preserved attribution, target/overlap behavior,
unique IDs, approved URLs, and constrained source categories.
