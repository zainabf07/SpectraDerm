## Signs and symptoms
The American Academy of Dermatology lists whiteheads, blackheads, pimples, nodules, and cysts among acne breakouts. Its page describes a whitehead as a raised white or flesh-colored blemish that can form when oil and dead skin cells plug a pore.

## Inflammation and surface context
The source explains that a nodule or cyst can develop when inflammation goes deep into the skin and may feel tender or painful. This is limited common-condition context for later retrieval.
