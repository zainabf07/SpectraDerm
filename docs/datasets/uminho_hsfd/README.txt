University of Minho Hyperspectral Images of Faces Database: UMINHO-HSFDREADMEThe University of Minho Hyperspectral Images of Faces Database (UMINHO-HSFD) is a curated and characterized database of hyperspectral imaging of human facial skin.CITATION ====================================================Any use of the UMINHO-HSFD should cite the following reference:Gomes, Andreia E.; Linhares, Joao M. M.; Nascimento, Sergio M. C. (2024). University of Minho Hyperspectral Faces Database: UMINHO-HSFD. figshare. Collection. https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.c.7163569OVERVIEW ==================================================UMINHO-HSFD consists of 29 hyperspectral images of human faces with diverse skin tones and sexes. Each hyperspectral image encompasses the entire face, comprising spectral reflectance from 400 nm to 720 nm in 10 nm steps (33 wavelengths) at every image pixel.Faces were positioned to minimize facial movements and to be optimized for the lighting conditions. Data acquisition took place in a controlled laboratory environment with special care taken to minimize non-skin features such as exposed teeth, hair, open eyes, beard, make-up, or accessories.These measures were ascertained with spectral measurements made with conventional local point and/or contact instruments. An analysis was conducted for both inter- and intra-group variations across different facial locations for each skin color group, revealing important local variations in the spectral reflectance profile that cannot be detected when using local point measuring devices.Keywords: hyperspectral imaging; skin color; human faces; facial color; facial hyperspectral imaging; facial skin reflectance; colorimetry; spectral reflectanceCONTENTS ================================================UMINHO-HSFD files are available to download and use, assuming the terms and conditions outlined described below. This collection contains the following files:“README” (this file): Instructions about UMINHO-HSFD such as hyperspectral images description, database use rules and citation and contact information, documentation, used terms, and other information.“faces_metadata” (PDF file): Metadata for each face including sex, age (in years), skin color classification based on the Fitzpatrick scale (from I to VI) and on the von Luschan scale (from 1 to 36), and the number of discernible colors assuming the CIE D65 illuminant and the CIE 2006 10 degrees cone-fundamental-based colorimetric observer. “faces_features” (PDF file): Visible facial features (moles, freckles, scars, and others) for each face. Features were intrinsic characteristics of each face and were not related to the acquisition process. “images_artifacts” (PDF file): Visible artifacts for each image, due to movement (blur or green colored areas) or due to unwanted visible elements (hair) present during the acquisition, only visible after image processing. “set-up_description” (PDF file): Details about the acquisition set-up. “files_metadata” (PDF file): Metadata for each face’s MAT and JPG files with file name, size, and dimension. Dimension was presented in pixel and wavelength units as HxWx? for MAT files and as HxW for JPG files, in which H, W, and ? correspond to the height, weight, and wavelength from 400 nm to 720 nm, respectively.“reflectance_files” (29 MAT files): Hyperspectral images (HxWx?) containing the spectral reflectance of each image pixel. H and W correspond to the height and the weight of the matrix in pixel units, and ? corresponds to the wavelength from 400 nm to 720 nm. Reflectance values of “0” are artificial and represent the faces’ surrounding black background.“RGB_files” (29 JPG files): RGB-colored images rendered from the hyperspectral images (available as m-files) assuming the CIE D65 illuminant and the CIE 2006 10 degrees cone-fundamental-based colorimetric observer. They were provided to be used as a visual reference. Images were redacted for identity protection.“ref2sRGB” (MAT file): Routine to convert the reflectance files into RGB images. AUTHORS ===================================================Andreia E.
