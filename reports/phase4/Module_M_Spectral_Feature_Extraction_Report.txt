SpectraDerm - Module M Spectral Feature Extraction Report
==========================================================

Module: M - Spectral Feature Extraction
Status: Technically completed

1. OBJECTIVE AND ARCHITECTURE ROLE
----------------------------------

Module M extracts deterministic, conventional numerical features from the
AI-estimated 31-band VIS spectral cube produced by Module I. It is a feature
layer for future classical feature fusion and machine-learning experiments;
it is not a diagnostic classifier. Module M operates on AI-estimated
reconstructed VIS spectral information, not direct hyperspectral measurements.

2. IMPLEMENTATION AND INPUT OUTPUT CONTRACT
--------------------------------------------

Implementation: src/spectraderm/features/spectral_features.py
Public API: extract_spectral_features(spectral_cube, wavelengths_nm=None,
mask=None) -> SpectralFeatureResult

The cube must be a finite numeric NumPy array with exact shape (H, W, 31) and
non-empty spatial dimensions. It is never transposed, resized, interpolated,
clipped, or repaired. Default wavelengths are exactly the 31 Hyper-Skin VIS
values: 400, 410, 420, 430, 440, 450, 460, 470, 480, 490, 500, 510, 520,
530, 540, 550, 560, 570, 580, 590, 600, 610, 620, 630, 640, 650, 660, 670,
680, 690, and 700 nm. Custom wavelength arrays must have exactly 31 finite,
strictly increasing, unique values; they are never silently sorted.

An optional finite numeric or boolean (H, W) mask selects an ROI. Without a
mask, the whole cube is selected. Empty and dimension-mismatched masks are
rejected. SpectralFeatureResult includes flat float features, feature groups,
the ROI regional-mean signature and aligned wavelengths, ROI metadata, and
warnings.

3. FEATURE DEFINITIONS
----------------------

Band intensities: for every available wavelength X, spectral_mean_X is the
mean ROI value and spectral_std_X is its population standard deviation.

Ratios: ratio_540_650, ratio_550_650, ratio_570_650, ratio_580_650,
ratio_600_650, and ratio_650_700 are mean(A) / mean(B). A zero or near-zero
denominator yields NaN plus a warning, never infinity.

Differences: difference_540_550, difference_550_570, difference_570_600,
difference_600_650, and difference_650_700 are mean(A) - mean(B), preserving
sign.

Slopes: slope_400_500, slope_500_600, slope_600_700, and slope_400_700 are
(mean(B) - mean(A)) / (B nm - A nm), using actual wavelength values.

Regional statistics: regional_spectral_mean, regional_spectral_std,
regional_spectral_median, regional_spectral_min, and regional_spectral_max
summarize all selected pixels over all 31 bands. spectral_signature is a
separate shape-(31,) array of regional mean values, index-aligned to
wavelengths_nm; it is deliberately not placed in the flat feature dictionary.

4. INVESTIGATIONAL PROXIES
--------------------------

vascular_related_spectral_proxy is (mean_540 - mean_580) /
(mean_540 + mean_580), returning NaN plus a warning for a near-zero
denominator. It is a visible-band spectral appearance pattern only.

pigmentation_related_spectral_proxy is mean_650 / mean_540, returning NaN
plus a warning for a near-zero denominator. It is a visible-band spectral
appearance pattern only.

Vascular-related features are investigational proxies and are not measurements
of hemoglobin concentration or inflammation. Pigmentation-related spectral
features are investigational proxies and are not direct measurements of
melanin concentration. Neither proxy is calibrated, diagnostic, or a
biological concentration measurement.

5. EXCLUSIONS
-------------

The current 400-700 nm output is not used to derive a water-related proxy.
No water content, water concentration, water absorption, or hydration feature
is provided because this visible-only range does not support that
interpretation. A structural/collagen-related proxy is not established by the
current spectral output and is excluded. No collagen concentration, structural
tissue score, or skin-structure score is provided.

6. NOTEBOOK AND TESTING
-----------------------

notebooks/Module_M_Spectral_Feature_Extraction.ipynb demonstrates local
feature extraction. It prefers data/interim/face_spectral_cube.npy and
explicitly transposes it once only when it is channel-first (31, H, W). If the
file is unavailable, it clearly creates a small synthetic demonstration cube.
It requires no Colab, GPU, PyTorch, or Hyper-Skin archive.

tests/test_spectral_features.py uses deterministic small synthetic cubes and
covers validation, canonical and invalid wavelengths, no sorting, intensity
mapping, ROI behavior, undefined ratios, differences, actual-nm slopes,
regional statistics, signature alignment, proxies, exclusions, determinism,
and result metadata. Final test results are recorded after validation.

Validation result: 31 Module M tests passed in 0.87 seconds. The requested
frozen Module I-L regression set produced 40 passed and 1 skipped in 1.82
seconds. The skipped test is the PyTorch-backed MST++ RGB-to-VIS integration
test; PyTorch remains intentionally unavailable in the local Windows
environment. No packages were installed.

7. RELATIONSHIP TO MODULES I THROUGH L
--------------------------------------

Module I supplies the estimated VIS cube. Module J validates reconstruction
quality where reference data are available; Module M does not reinterpret that
validation. Module K visualizes estimated spectral cubes; Module M calculates
numerical features without changing visualization behavior. Module L supplies
independent conventional RGB features. Modules G and H may supply an ROI mask,
but Module M performs no segmentation or localization.

8. LIMITATIONS AND SAFETY BOUNDARIES
------------------------------------

Spectral features do not establish disease presence or absence. They do not
diagnose disease, produce disease probability, or establish inflammation,
redness, melanin, blood oxygenation, collagen, water, lesions, cancer, or any
other medical condition. Results depend on the reconstructed cube quality,
illumination and camera inputs to reconstruction, the supplied ROI, and the
limitations of AI-estimated VIS information.
