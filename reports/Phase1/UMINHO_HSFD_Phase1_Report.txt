IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
---------------------
UMINHO-HSFD contains only 29 subjects.
Skin-tone groups are strongly imbalanced.
The RGB images are rendered from hyperspectral reflectance
and should therefore not be treated as an independently captured
RGB modality.
Zero reflectance values represent artificial black background.
The dataset is primarily suitable for spectral/skin analysis
and validation rather than disease-classifier training.
